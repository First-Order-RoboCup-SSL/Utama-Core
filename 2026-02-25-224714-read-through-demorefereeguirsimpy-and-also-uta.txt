  ---
  Changes

  state_machine.py

  Auto-advance 2 (PREPARE_KICKOFF → NORMAL_START) now requires both:
  - prepare_duration_seconds elapsed (gives robots time to move into formation)
  - At least one attacker inside the centre circle (_kicker_in_centre_circle)

  _kicker_in_centre_circle: resolves "attacker" from PREPARE_KICKOFF_YELLOW/BLUE
   vs my_team_is_yellow, then checks hypot(robot.x, robot.y) ≤ 
  center_circle_radius.

  New auto-advance 3 (DIRECT_FREE_ → NORMAL_START)* — _free_kick_ready checks:
  1. All defending robots ≥ 0.5 m from ball
  2. Closest attacking robot ≤ 0.3 m from ball (kicker in position)

  Both use rule-compliant logic — during DIRECT_FREE_* the attacking team is
  already allowed to approach the ball per the SSL rules, so the kicker can
  drive up to it; the trigger is the defending side being clear.

  Auto-advance 4 (NORMAL_START → FORCE_START) now covers both kickoff and free
  kick timeouts (same 10 s window).

  Free kick full sequence

  STOP  (all robots radially clear ball)
    → auto: DIRECT_FREE_*  (kicker approaches ball, defenders stay back)
    → auto: NORMAL_START   (kicker ≤0.3 m from ball + defenders ≥0.5 m)
    → [10 s window]
    → auto: FORCE_START    (if ball hasn't moved)
